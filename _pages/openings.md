# Join us!

We are always looking for talented and motivated students and postdocs with an interest in cutting-edge experimental research in quantum science and technology. Please contact [Prof. Simon L. Cornish](mailto:s.l.cornish@durham.ac.uk) to enquire about current opportunities.

### Current openings for PhD students

Fully funded PhD studentships (fees and stipend) are available on the following projects to start in October 2027:

#### 1. Quantum computing with atoms and molecules in optical tweezers ([Tweezers lab]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/tweezers))

#### 2. Quantum spin dynamics with ultracold polar molecules  ([Microscope lab]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/microscope))

Other projects may also be available through competitive funding applications (e.g. Faculty of Science SCOPE Doctoral Studentships).

### Current openings for Postdoctoral Researchers

We have no current vacancies, but are always interested in hearing from potential applicants with a passion for experimental research in AMO physics.

### Support for Postdoctoral Fellowships

We welcome approaches from postdoctoral researchers interested in applying for independent research fellowships to be hosted in Durham. We have a strong track record in supporting successful applications to a range of schemes and encourage candidates considering applying for any of the following fellowships to contact [Prof. Simon L. Cornish](mailto:s.l.cornish@durham.ac.uk): the [Royal Society University Research Fellowship](https://royalsociety.org/grants/university-research/), the [Royal Society Dorothy Hodgkin Fellowship](https://royalsociety.org/grants/dorothy-hodgkin-fellowship/), the [Royal Society Newton International Fellowship](https://royalsociety.org/grants/newton-international/) and the [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships).
